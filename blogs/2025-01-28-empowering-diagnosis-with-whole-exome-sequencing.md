WHAT IS WHOLE EXOME SEQUENCING? Navigating the complexities of genetic and rare conditions from diagnosis to treatment can be challenging, especially when answers feel out of reach. Determining the genetic cause of a patient’s condition is often crucial to effective medical management, targeted treatment, and informed genetic counseling.
